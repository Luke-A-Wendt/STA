\subsubsection{Complex Paravector-Valued Maps}
A generic complex paravector-valued map is formed from real scalar and
vector functions without imposing any physical interpretation:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\pv{Z}(t,\vct{r}) &:= \bigl(a(t,\vct{r})+i\,b(t,\vct{r})\bigr)\\
&\quad + \bigl(\vct{A}(t,\vct{r})+i\,\vct{B}(t,\vct{r})\bigr)\cdot\sigv.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:complex-paravector-map}
\end{equation}
Its domain and the coefficient-function types are
\begin{align*}
a,b &: \R\times\R^3 \to \R,\\
\vct{A},\vct{B} &: \R\times\R^3 \to \R^3,\\
\pv{Z} &: \R\times\R^3 \to \C+\C^3.
\end{align*}
