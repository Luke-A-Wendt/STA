\subsection{Complex Fields}\label{subsec:differential-complex-fields}
For a generic complex paravector field, the differential operator and field
are written as
\begin{align*}
\pv{\partial} &= \partial_t+\gradv\cdot\sigv,\\
\pv{F} &= (a+ib)+(\vct{A}+i\vct{B})\cdot\sigv.
\end{align*}
The component fields are assumed to have the domains
\begin{align*}
a &: \R\times\R^3\to\R, &
\vct{A} &: \R\times\R^3\to\R^3,\\
b &: \R\times\R^3\to\R, &
\vct{B} &: \R\times\R^3\to\R^3,
\end{align*}
so that
\begin{equation*}
\pv{F}:\R\times\R^3\to\C+\C^3.
\end{equation*}
